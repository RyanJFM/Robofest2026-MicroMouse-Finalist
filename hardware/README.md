# Hardware

## Main Components

* Arduino Nano
* TB6612FNG motor driver
* 2 × N20 300 RPM 6 V motors with magnetic encoders
* ICM-20602 gyroscope
* 4 × Sharp GP2Y0A51SK0F IR distance sensors
* HC-06 Bluetooth module
* 2 × 3.7 V LiPo batteries connected in series
* Green and blue status LEDs
* Custom 3D-printed chassis
* Hand-soldered dot-board electronics

## Sensor Arrangement

The robot uses four Sharp IR sensors:

* Front-left
* Front-right
* Left-side wall sensor
* Right-side wall sensor

The front sensors are used for front-wall detection and user gesture input.

The side sensors are used primarily for left/right wall detection and motion correction.

## Power

The robot uses two 3.7 V LiPo cells connected in series, providing a nominal 7.4 V supply.

Additional local capacitors are used for sensor and signal stability.

## Mechanical Design

The chassis is custom 3D printed and designed around the compact Micromouse form factor.
